What we repair

Most doors we see fail in one of six ways

Ordered by how often the phone rings for it. The first three make up the bulk of our week.

01

Spring replacement

A loud bang from the garage, then a door that will not lift. Springs are rated in cycles and the Valley heat does not help them last. We replace the pair and balance the door.

See pricing
02

Opener repair

The motor runs but the door stays put, usually a stripped gear. We test the board, gear and safety eyes first. When the repair gets close to the cost of a new unit, we say so.

See pricing
03

Off-track and cables

A roller jumps the rail and the door sits crooked. Running the opener from there bends panels and turns a small fix into a big one. Unplug it and call.

Is it safe to use
04

Bottom seal replacement

Arizona sun cooks the rubber until it splits, and then dust, water and scorpions come straight in. A fresh seal takes under an hour.

Book a seal swap
05

Tune-up and rollers

A door that sounds like it is coming apart is usually just tired hardware. A tune-up takes the strain off the opener and buys you years.

See pricing
06

New door installation

When the panels are bent past repair, a new door is the cheaper answer. You get the model, the insulation rating and the install date in writing.

See pricing
